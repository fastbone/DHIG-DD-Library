Corpus access

Files copied onto a mounted volume from outside the app — a host shell, an rsync feed, another container — often arrive owned by root and unreadable here. Such folders are skipped during ingest, so a full data room can index as zero documents. This checks every configured root and repairs what it is allowed to.
